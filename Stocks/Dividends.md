Fidelity gives you a few levers to control what happens when dividends and capital gains hit your account.

### The Core Options

**Reinvest** deposits the dividend back into the same security as additional shares (or fractional shares). This is the default for most accounts and is how DRIP (Dividend Reinvestment Plan) works. Fidelity does this at no commission.

**Deposit to core position** sends the cash into your settlement account (typically SPAXX or FZFXX for brokerage, or FDIC cash for CMA accounts) where it sits as cash. Good if you want to deploy it manually elsewhere.

**Deposit to another account** lets you redirect dividends to a different linked Fidelity account, like sweeping taxable dividends into a Roth or a separate cash management account.

### Where You Set This

You can configure it at two levels:

- **Account level** sets the default behavior for all positions in that account.
- **Position level** overrides the account default for a specific holding. So you can reinvest most things but take cash from one particular ETF.

To get there: Accounts and Trade > Account Features > Brokerage and Trading > Dividend and Capital Gains.

### Mutual Funds vs. ETFs/Stocks

Mutual funds (including Fidelity's own index funds) have a separate dividend election from stocks and ETFs. You'll see them listed independently in the dividend settings UI. Don't assume changing one changes the other.

### Tax Considerations Worth Knowing

Reinvested dividends are still taxable in the year received in a taxable account. Fidelity will report them on your 1099-DIV regardless. The reinvested shares also establish a new cost basis lot, which matters when you eventually sell. Fidelity tracks this automatically and will report it on your 1099-B.

### Practical Notes

If you hold a position in multiple account types (taxable, IRA, 401k), the election is per account. A 401k managed through Fidelity may not expose the same granular controls depending on your plan rules. IRAs generally give you full control the same as a brokerage account.
