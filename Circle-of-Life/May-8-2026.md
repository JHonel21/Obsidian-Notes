- Children must see a PCP within 30 days of placement 
- Ensure Medicaid is accepted at all places prior to going
- If medical providers request info for responsible payer on excess costs, list DHS contact info and not ours
- Good idea to add Circle of Care numbers into our phones
- Must get child enrolled in school within 3 days of placement
	- If the school gives a hard time about not having all the documents, say they are a foster child and know OK law states they get to bypass typical enrollment requirements 
		- If that still has problems, then tell them to speak with Circle of Care or DHS case worker
- Injuries and Incident reports need to be reported within 24 hours via phone call
	- Any injuries that are significant should be reported
	- Incidents would include: running away, sexually acting out, police involvement, ingestion of drugs or harmful substances, major behavioral issue, urgent care visits, ER visits, or hospitalization 
- Be willing to take calls at all times
- Questions to ask when considering placement:
	- Age
	- Why in foster care
	- What is the plan for visits with bio parents
	- Do they have siblings, what are their names/ages, and where are they placed 
	- When is the next sibling visit
	- Medical problems, allergies, medications
	- Do they eat or sleep well
	- What is the eventual goal/plan
- Further questions to ask post placement acceptance:
	- When to see doctor
	- Last physical or dental exam
	- Names of doctors and dentist, and location
	- Existing medical/dental visits scheduled 
	- Medication supplies, time on the meds, and where/how to refill
	- Next family court date
	- Favorite or least favorite foods
	- Full legal name and preferred name
	- DHS worker and supervisor name and phone number
	- Closest people to the child and their relationship 
	- Are they in school
- We need to give a 7-day notice if requesting removal of child, unless it’s emergent
- Giving a child a snack bowl at all times is a very good way to build trust and make them feel safe
- They use Bill.com for reimbursements and fiscal year ends in June
	- Receipts are required for reimbursement 
	- Amounts are allotted quarterly
