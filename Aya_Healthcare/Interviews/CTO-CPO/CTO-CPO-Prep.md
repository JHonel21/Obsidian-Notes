Question 1: “Walk me through how you would lead a pre-sales engagement from first conversation to signed SOW.”

Strong Answer

I’d structure it in four stages.

First is discovery, where I focus on understanding the problem, constraints, and what success looks like. I’m particularly focused on where current processes or systems are breaking down and what impact that’s having operationally or financially.

Second is solution structuring. I translate that into a clear approach with defined workstreams, a delivery model, and an initial staffing plan. This is where I’m ensuring the solution is both technically sound and realistic to deliver.

Third is proposal development, where I formalize scope, define deliverables, outline timelines, and align staffing with cost. I’m very intentional about assumptions and scope boundaries to avoid downstream risk.

Finally, I support executive discussions to position the solution, address concerns, and refine based on feedback before converting that into a final SOW.

In my current role, I’ve done a version of this from the client side, evaluating solutions, structuring recommendations, and ensuring what’s proposed is actually executable within our environment.
