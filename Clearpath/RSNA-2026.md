## 3. Engagement & Lead Capture

Our goal is to engage every attendee who visits our booth and capture meaningful information from every conversation.

### 3.1 Scan every attendee

Anyone who engages with us at the booth should be scanned. If you're speaking with someone and don't have access to a scanner, make sure another team member scans them before they leave. Please take good notes so we can do proper follow-up.

The following team members will have the badge scanner app on their devices: Linh, Payton, Tara, Marissa, Jason, Jake.

### 3.2 Capture meaningful conversation notes

We will speak with hundreds of people throughout the conference. Capturing the details of those conversations is critical to ensuring we have actionable follow-up after RSNA.

Please enter notes directly into the lead capture app. Important details include:

- Action items and next steps
- Who we should contact following RSNA
- Their role and key decision-makers
- Current systems (PACS, RIS, billing platforms, EMR, etc.)
- Current technology and workflows
- Competitors or solutions currently deployed or under evaluation
- Release of Information (ROI) vendor
- Patient and imaging study volumes
- Operational challenges and pain points
- Areas of interest and potential opportunities
- Expected timing and next steps

Don't just capture a name and company. We want to understand the opportunity and have enough context to continue the conversation after the conference.

### 3.3 Booth Roles: Greeters, Presenters & Triage

We have three designated demo stations in the booth: Kamil, Lauren, and Linh. These individuals will lead presentations and more in-depth conversations.

The booth should operate like an assembly line. Everyone should actively engage attendees walking through the aisle, invite them into the booth, scan their badges, offer them our promotional items, and start a conversation before transitioning them to Lauren, Linh, or Kamil for a more detailed discussion.

If all three presenters are occupied, use the talk tracks in this guide to keep the conversation going until someone becomes available.

Tara will lead booth triage when Lauren, Linh, and Kamil are presenting or otherwise engaged. She will help determine who should handle incoming opportunities and ensure attendees are not left waiting. Tara will also oversee rescheduling/scheduling if we run into issues with overflow.

### 3.4 Stay Engaged During Scheduled Meetings

We have approximately 80 scheduled meetings across four days, many of which will occupy our presenters for extended periods.

During these times, it is especially important that everyone else remains actively engaged with attendees. Please do not spend time on personal phones or computers. Our investment in RSNA is significant, and we need everyone focused on making the most of the opportunity.

One easy conversation starter is our YETI giveaway. Encourage attendees to scan their badges to enter! Or give them a million dollars!

## 4. Clearpath Talk Track

**The most important message to memorize:**

> "We empower healthcare facilities to share images and records digitally across the entire healthcare enterprise, including patients, legal requestors, providers, and more. We've developed and patented our own technology that can connect to virtually any PACS in less than 30 minutes, along with pre-built integrations with leading EMR, RIS, and billing platforms."

### Start by understanding who you're speaking with

Pause and ask about their role within the organization. This will help you determine where to take the conversation. For example:

- Have they heard of Clearpath before?
- Do they oversee operations or HIM?
- Do they have insight into legal or ROI workflows?
- Are they involved in IT or technical integrations? If so, they may need to speak with Daniel or Kamil.
- What are the biggest challenges they're experiencing today?

The goal is to understand their needs before launching into a product pitch.

Use the brochure to walk through our three primary modules:

### LegalConnect

> "Our legal module enables facilities to receive and manage legal requests digitally, eliminating the need for faxes, phone calls, emails, and physical mail. Staff can fulfill a legal request in just a few minutes. Clearpath automates invoicing, collects payment, and releases images and records once payment is received. The majority of legal requestors we work with are already using our platform. Our legal solution enables you to optimize revenue collection on these requests. Many of our customers reallocate that increased revenue to fund other internal projects."

### PatientConnect

> "Our automated patient module provides patients with automatic access to their images and reports as they leave the facility in your existing patient portal, or Clearpath's platform."

Patients can:

1. View their images and reports.
2. Share images and reports with other physicians and family members.
3. Better understand their reports through our enhanced, patient-friendly medical terminology feature.

### ProviderConnect

> "Our provider module enables facilities to share images and reports with other providers, anywhere and anytime. Providers can login to a portal to use enhanced features. Many providers also don't want another login, so we have a login-less workflow that meets every provider where they want to be met. Providers can query backwards to compare priors on patients and perform many other steps. Everything is tied to NPI so providers have a consolidated workflow. This also permits other delegates like admins and surgical assistants to self-manage and self-administer in the same NPI account, further streamlining access."

### FacilityConnect

> "Facility Connect empowers facilities to seamlessly exchange images in connected and non-connected workflows. For facilities with high volumes of image exchange, we recommend our fully automated workflow. This allows facilities to automatically share images and reports, as well as receive outside images directly into Clearpath and their PACS.
> 
> We can ingest outside images using MRN matching and AI, and automate the retrieval of relevant prior imaging studies from the facilities you frequently request them from. This can dramatically reduce the administrative burden and FTE time required to manage image exchange, especially compared to legacy workflows."

- Ask whether they use Imagine. We have a deep integration and extensive capabilities for legal image releases through Imagine.

### Customer Validation

## 5. Other Important Talking Points

- **Our Differentiation:** We are the first and only platform that bridges HIM and Radiology workflows, enabling organizations to eliminate discs across the entire healthcare enterprise.
- **Legal Pricing:** A low, flat fee per transaction. Facilities retain the majority of the revenue while benefiting from faster payment collection through Clearpath.
- **Patient & Provider Pricing:** These products are subscription-based. Pricing depends on study volumes, preferred storage requirements, and other factors. We would need to understand their environment before providing a quote.
- **When Someone Says They Already Use PowerShare:** Respond with: "Approximately 95% of the facilities we partner with already have an image-sharing solution in place. They still need Clearpath because significant workflow gaps and opportunities for automation remain with those legacy solutions."
- **Don't Lead With Eliminating Discs:** Most facilities already have some form of image-sharing solution, so eliminating discs alone is not a compelling differentiator. Focus on automation, workflow improvements, and the broader capabilities of our platform.
- **Minimize Device Use:** Please keep personal phone and computer use to a minimum. We have invested significantly in being here, and our focus should be on the people visiting our booth.

## 6. Who to Avoid & How to Handle Certain Attendees

Please use good judgment when engaging with the following groups. When in doubt, refer them to Lauren or Linh.

### Canadian Attendees

PocketHealth has a strong presence in Canada, particularly in Ontario. We want to be mindful of sharing proprietary information with potential competitors or their representatives, even customers. We have customers that feed us intel.

If a Canadian facility requests marketing materials or asks for detailed product information, politely explain that our current technology and services are designed for the U.S. market and are not currently applicable to Canada. Do not distribute brochures or share detailed information.
