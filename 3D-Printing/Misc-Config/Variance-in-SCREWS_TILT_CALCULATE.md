Acceptable: Within 00:15 (quarter turn) of reference

Good: Within 00:10

Exceptional: All screws at 00:05 or better (basically 00:00 to 00:05)

For the Ender 5 S1, getting everything within 00:10 is perfectly fine for excellent prints. Chasing absolute 00:00 on every screw can be frustrating due to bed/frame flex.
