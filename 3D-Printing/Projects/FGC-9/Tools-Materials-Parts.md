### Materials
- [ ] Round steel stock (18mm,3mm)
	• 1x 50mm long and 1x 216mm long or one piece longer than 300mm, 18mm diameter +-0.1mm (Ideally you'll have multiple of those pieces in case you make mistakes or want to make multiple bolts. So buy a round steel stock bar of 18mm diameter that is longer than 300mm at least, and then cut it with your angle grinder into pieces as you need it)
	• 1x 200mm long or as a longer piece, 3mm diameter +-0.01mm
	• IMPORTANT FOR BOTH: The round steel stock has to be non-hardened, so no tool steel for example! Uncoated! Use carbon steel (regular steel)! Any other steels are not recommended. For the Ø3mm stock, stainless steel is acceptable.
