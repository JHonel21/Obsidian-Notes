Proposal + SOW QA Checklist (SA Standard)

1. Problem Alignment & Value

- Is the problem statement explicitly tied to client pain and validated through discovery?
- Are success criteria defined in measurable terms?
- Is there clear linkage between:

- Problem → solution → outcomes → value?

- Are we solving the right problem, not just the stated request?

2. Scope Definition

- Is scope explicitly defined and bounded?
- Are in-scope and out-of-scope items clearly separated?
- Are all major deliverables listed and unambiguous?
- Are dependencies and assumptions clearly stated?
- Is there a defined mechanism for scope change control?

3. Delivery Approach & Framework

- Is the delivery approach broken into logical phases?
- Are phases aligned to:

- Discovery
- Design
- Build / Implement
- Validate / Stabilize

- Is there a clear execution methodology?
- Are handoffs between phases clearly defined?
- Does the approach align with realistic timelines?

4. Staffing Model & Capacity

- Are roles clearly defined (not just titles)?
- Is staffing aligned to each phase of delivery?
- Are utilization expectations realistic?
- Is there a balance between:

- Senior oversight
- Execution resources

- Are assumptions about client resources explicitly defined?
- Does the staffing model support the proposed timeline?

5. Client vs. Aya Responsibilities

- Is there a clear RACI-style delineation of responsibilities?
- Are client dependencies realistic and acknowledged?
- Is it clear what Aya delivers vs. what the client must provide?
- Are risks identified if client responsibilities are not met?

6. Technical Feasibility

- Is the solution technically sound given:

- Existing systems
- Integration requirements
- Data availability

- Are integration points clearly defined (e.g., Epic, FHIR, HL7)?
- Are there any architectural gaps or risks?
- Are performance and scalability considerations addressed?

7. Data & Integration Considerations

- Are data sources clearly identified?
- Is data movement (inbound/outbound) defined?
- Are data quality and governance risks addressed?
- Are assumptions about data access and availability stated?
- Is there a clear plan for reporting or analytics outputs?

8. Timeline & Milestones

- Is the timeline broken into milestones with deliverables?
- Are timelines realistic given scope and staffing?
- Are dependencies accounted for?
- Is there buffer for risk or unknowns?

9. Risk Assessment

- Are key risks explicitly identified?
- Are risks categorized (technical, operational, commercial)?
- Are mitigation strategies defined?
- Are there early warning indicators?
- Is there a clear escalation path?

10. Commercial Alignment

- Does the scope align with pricing and effort?
- Is there risk of underestimating effort or margin erosion?
- Are assumptions tied to cost drivers?
- Is the engagement financially viable?
- Does the proposal align with sales positioning?

11. Assumptions & Constraints

- Are all assumptions clearly documented?
- Are constraints acknowledged (time, access, resources)?
- Are assumptions realistic and testable?
- Is there a mechanism to revisit assumptions if needed?

12. Deliverables & Acceptance Criteria

- Are deliverables clearly defined and measurable?
- Is there a defined definition of done?
- Are acceptance criteria included?
- Is there alignment on how deliverables will be validated?

13. Governance & Communication

- Is there a governance model defined?

- Steering committee
- Working sessions
- Status reporting

- Are reporting cadences defined?
- Is stakeholder engagement clearly outlined?
- Are decision-making processes defined?

14. Change Control & Scope Management

- Is there a defined change control process?
- Are scope changes tied to:

- Impact on timeline
- Impact on cost

- Is there a clear escalation path for scope changes?

15. Transition to Delivery

- Is there a clear handoff from proposal to delivery?
- Are roles and responsibilities maintained post-contract?
- Is there alignment with delivery teams?
- Is documentation sufficient for execution?

How to Use This in the Interview

If asked about QA, you can say:

I approach QA across both proposals and SOWs as a structured validation process that looks at scope, staffing, delivery feasibility, and commercial alignment.

I focus on ensuring that what is being proposed is actually deliverable, that responsibilities 

between Aya and the client are clear, and that the commercial structure aligns with the effort required.

This helps prevent scope gaps, delivery risk, and margin issues before anything is **finalized**
