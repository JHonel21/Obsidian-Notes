## Questions (in order)

1. What's prompting the search for this role right now?
   - If he's vague ("We're just looking to strengthen the practice at the principal level."): 
	   - That makes sense. Is this a net new position or are you backfilling a gap in coverage?
   - If he's direct ("Honestly we haven't had anyone operating at the level you were since you left."): 
	   - I appreciate you saying that. What specifically has been hardest to cover?
   - If he references growth or a new initiative ("We're expanding into some larger enterprise accounts and need someone who can lead those engagements."): 
	   - Is there a specific segment or platform focus driving that, or is it more broadly across the book of business?

2. How has the implementation practice evolved since I left?
   - If things have changed positively ("We've added headcount, refined some processes, brought in some new tooling."): 
	   - Good to hear. Where do you feel the practice still has room to mature?
   - If things have been flat or struggled ("It's been a transition period. We're still finding our footing in some areas."): 
	   - That context is helpful. It sounds like there's an opportunity to help shape where things go from here, which is actually the kind of role I'd find most compelling.
   - If he deflects ("It's evolved a lot, hard to summarize quickly."): 
	   - Fair enough. Maybe a better question is what you'd want this role to change or accelerate in the next 12 months.

3. What does success look like for whoever steps into this position in the first year?
   - If he describes execution ("Leading our most complex implementations, getting clients live, hitting timelines."): 
	   - Is the expectation primarily individual delivery or is there a team enablement component, things like SOPs, training, that kind of infrastructure?
   - If he describes leadership ("Building out frameworks, training the team, being a go-to resource for escalations."): 
	   - That aligns with how I'd want to contribute. Is that scope reflected in how the role is titled and leveled?
   - If he's unclear ("Honestly we'd want you to help define that."): 
	   - I'm open to that, but I'd want some guardrails going in so we're aligned on what authority and ownership actually look like day one.

4. Based on what you're describing, it sounds like the need is less about individual project delivery and more about guiding the most complex engagements and building repeatable frameworks. Is that accurate?
   - If he agrees ("Yes exactly, we need someone who can operate above the project level."): 
	   - Good. Then I'd want to make sure the title reflects that. Principal Consultant reads as senior IC. Something like Implementation Solutions Architect or Principal Solutions Architect would better match what you're describing.
   - If he hedges ("The title is what it is internally but the work is what you're describing."): 
	   - I understand there are internal constraints, but title matters for how the role is perceived by clients and the team. Is there any flexibility there or is that a fixed variable?

5. What does the reporting structure look like for this role?
   - If it's direct to him or a director: No follow-up needed beyond confirming who owns decisions on scope and priorities.
   - If it's layered under someone you don't know: Can you tell me more about that person's background and how they think about the practice?
   - If it's unclear: Understanding the reporting line matters to me because it shapes how much autonomy the role actually has. Can you clarify that before we go further?

1. What's the comp band and is there flexibility on title to reflect an architectural function rather than a consulting one? 
   - If the band is competitive: That's in the right range. I'd want to finalize scope and title before committing, but that clears the first hurdle.
   - If the band is low: I appreciate the transparency. That's below where I'd need to land given where I am now and what I'd be stepping into. Is there flexibility or is that the ceiling?
   - If he won't share the band: I want to be respectful of everyone's time including yours. If we're not in the same range it's better to know now. Can you give me at least a ballpark?
