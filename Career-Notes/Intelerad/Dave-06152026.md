Questions to ask, in order

	1.	“What’s prompting the search for this role right now?”
	2.	“How has the implementation practice evolved since I left?”
	3.	“What does success look like for whoever steps into this position in the first year?”
	4.	“Based on what you’re describing, it sounds like the need is less about individual project delivery and more about guiding the most complex engagements and building repeatable frameworks. Is that accurate?”
	5.	“What does the reporting structure look like for this role?”
	6.	“Is there a defined product feedback channel, or would that be part of what this person helps build?”
	7.	“What’s the comp band and is there flexibility on title to reflect an architectural function rather than a consulting one?”

	•	How many go-lives you led or that you felt underrecognized before leaving
